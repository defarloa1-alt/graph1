## Official US Military Journals (All Free, All Open Access)

These are government publications — fully free by law — and carry significant doctrinal weight:[[libguides.nps](https://libguides.nps.edu/milpubs/jrs)]

- **Parameters** (US Army War College Quarterly) — grand strategy, Landpower, national security; the most academically rigorous of the service journals
    
- **Military Review** (US Army Command & General Staff College) — operational and tactical focus; available in English, Spanish, Portuguese
    
- **Joint Force Quarterly** (National Defense University / Joint Chiefs) — joint doctrine, coalition warfare, unified command operations
    
- **Naval War College Review** — maritime strategy and international security
    
- **Air & Space Power Journal** (US Air Force) — airpower doctrine and strategy
    
- **Journal of Advanced Military Studies (JAMS)** (Marine Corps University) — peer-reviewed; strong on expeditionary warfare theory[[libguides.nps](https://libguides.nps.edu/milpubs/jrs)]
    
- **PRISM** (Irregular Warfare Center / NDU) — complex operations, stability, irregular warfare
    

All of these are accessible at **Army University Press** (armyupress.army.mil) which acts as a free aggregator.[[armyupress.army](https://www.armyupress.army.mil/Resources/)]

## Open-Access Academic Journals

- **Journal of Military and Strategic Studies** (jmss.org) — fully open access, peer-reviewed, covers strategy theory through current Vol. 24[[jmss](https://jmss.org/)]
    
- **Homeland Security Affairs** (NPS) — free, covers civil-military and security policy[[libguides.nps](https://libguides.nps.edu/milpubs/jrs)]
    
- **CTC Sentinel** (Combating Terrorism Center, West Point) — terrorism and irregular warfare; free monthly[[libguides.nps](https://libguides.nps.edu/milpubs/jrs)]
    
- **Combating Threats Exchange (CTX)** (NPS Global ECCO) — free, multilingual, irregular warfare focus[[libguides.nps](https://libguides.nps.edu/milpubs/jrs)]

## For the Military SFA Specifically

The most directly useful for training against **strategy theory and doctrine** (not just news):

1. **Military Strategy Magazine** — pure strategy theory, closest to what the SFA needs for predicate vocabulary
    
2. **Parameters** — the gold standard for US strategic studies; back issues freely searchable
    
3. **War on the Rocks** — living discourse; keeps the SFA's analytical framework current
    
4. **Army University Press resources hub** — single entry point to nearly all US military official publications[[armyupress.army](https://www.armyupress.army.mil/Resources/)]
    

The **Air University Library Index to Military Periodicals (AULIMP)** is also worth knowing — it's a free citation index covering ~200 military periodicals, useful if you want the SFA to be able to search across publications by topic.[[libguides.nps](https://libguides.nps.edu/milpubs/jrs)]
